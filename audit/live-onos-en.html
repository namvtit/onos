Ask our team ↗
Can I start with a small order?+

Yes. DTG and sublimation orders can start from a single unit. Custom blanks and made-to-measure uniforms have minimum quantities agreed for each project.

What artwork files do you need?+

Send vector artwork (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF), or a transparent PNG at 300 DPI at the final print size. Our team checks the file before it moves into production.

Can we approve a sample first?+

Yes. Our Me Linh plant has a dedicated sampling line. For larger orders, we recommend agreeing a sample before the full production run.

How do we confirm pricing and lead time?+

Share the product, design, expected quantity and shipping destination. We will review the production requirements and discuss pricing, timing and shipping with you before you proceed.
